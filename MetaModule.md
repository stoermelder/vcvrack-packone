## PackOne for MetaModule

Selected modules are available on the [4ms MetaModule](https://4mscompany.com/p.php?p=990). The latest development build is available [here](https://github.com/stoermelder/vcvrack-packone/releases/tag/Nightly-MetaModule).

[4ROUNDS](https://github.com/stoermelder/vcvrack-packone/blob/v2/docs/fourrounds/FourRounds.md): randomizer for up to 16 input signals to create 15 output signals  
[BOLT](https://github.com/stoermelder/vcvrack-packone/blob/v2/docs/bolt/Bolt.md): polyphonic CV-modulateable boolean functions  
[HIVE](https://github.com/stoermelder/vcvrack-packone/blob/v2/docs/maze/Hive.md): 4 channel sequencer running on a 2-dimensional hexagonal grid, similar to [MAZE](https://github.com/stoermelder/vcvrack-packone/blob/v2/docs/maze/Maze.md)  
[MAZE](https://github.com/stoermelder/vcvrack-packone/blob/v2/docs/maze/Maze.md): 4 channel sequencer running on a 2-dimensional grid  
[MIDI-STEP](https://github.com/stoermelder/vcvrack-packone/blob/v2/docs/midi/MidiStep.md): utility for relative modes of endless knobs on your MIDI controller such as Arturia Beatstep  
[ORBIT](https://github.com/stoermelder/vcvrack-packone/blob/v2/docs/orbit/Orbit.md): a polyphonic stereo field spreader  
[PILE](https://github.com/stoermelder/vcvrack-packone/blob/v2/docs/pile/Pile.md): utility which translates increment triggers or decrement triggers in an absolute voltage, especially useful with MIDI-STEP  
[RAW](https://github.com/stoermelder/vcvrack-packone/blob/v2/docs/raw/Raw.md): a digital effect based on the dynamics of bistable systems
